Invigorating Physical Development

With 600+ PE teachers, we promote age-appropriate sports through the Narayana Premier League and Sports Achievement Reward Policy, fostering fitness, talent and teamwork.

nSports: Shaping Champions for Life

At Narayana Schools, we believe true education extends beyond academics. nSports, our age-appropriate physical education program, promotes holistic physical and mental growth. Through expert coaching, inclusive participation and team-based activities, it builds fitness, confidence and life skills like leadership, discipline and teamwork - preparing students for success on and off the field.

A Culture of Sporting Excellence

Narayana Schools promote sports as key to student growth. With expert coaches and diverse disciplines, our students excel at inter-school, state and national levels, earning laurels while building character and determination.

Summer Sports Camp

Our Summer Sports Camp offers aspiring athletes dynamic holiday training. Designed for all skill levels, it helps young enthusiasts enhance skills, fitness and resilience under expert coaching.

Narayana Premier League (NPL)

The Narayana Premier League (NPL) is a three-month sports fest that fuels school spirit. Students compete across grades, houses and zones in events from cricket to chess, celebrating teamwork, dedication and the unstoppable Narayana spirit.

Rewarding Sporting Achievements

To promote excellence, Narayana Schools introduced the Sports Achievement Reward Policy, a financial incentive program that recognizes and rewards outstanding student athletes, motivating them to reach new heights.
