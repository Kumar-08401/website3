NEET Answer Key and Solutions

NEET (UG) is conducted by the National Testing Agency (NTA) as a common and uniform National Eligibility-cum-Entrance Test for admission to undergraduate medical courses including MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BUMS, BSMS, BHMS, B.Sc. (H) Nursing and also serves eligibility for certain foreign medical/dental admissions and BVSc & AH seats under VCI quota.

Post-Exam Process

Answer Key & Challenges:

Provisional answer key, OMR sheets, and recorded responses will be published on National Testing Agency's website. Candidates can challenge the answer key within the given window by paying ₹200 per question (non-refundable).

Final Result:

The final answer key will be released before results, and the final result with All India Rank (AIR) will be declared based on it. No re-checking or re-evaluation is allowed. (https://neet.nta.nic.in)

PwD / PwBD provisions

Things to Remember

Keep copies of your Admit Card, OMR sheet, and Score Card - available for a limited period on https://neet.nta.nic.in
OMR sheet and Score Card also stored on DigiLocker - access via your DigiLocker account
NTA communicates only through the official website, registered email, and SMS - do not rely on unofficial sources
Qualifying NEET does not guarantee admission - seat allotment depends on AIR, eligibility, medical fitness, and other such criteria prescribed by the Government of India.
Check your state counselling authority's website regularly for State quota updates
For grievances or queries, contact NTA only through official channels at neet.nta.nic.in
