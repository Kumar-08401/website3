Schools

Narayana Schools focuses on an age-appropriate curriculum and serves three core age groups through distinct educational stages: kindergarten, primary and secondary. Each stage is thoughtfully designed to support every phase of a child's growth. The curriculum blends fundamental knowledge with critical thinking, enabling students to confidently understand and apply concepts using cutting-edge facilities.

Kindergarten

Narayana's eKidz programme provides personalised attention in a safe environment for students from Nursery to PP2.

Primary

With the eChamps programme, Narayana lays a strong academic and extracurricular foundation for students in Classes 1–5.

Secondary

Narayana provides various programmes for Classes 6-10, including eTechno, which has an integrated CBSE curriculum with an additional focus on competitive exams and the Olympiad programme (available in select branches), which focuses on preparing students to excel in various national and international Olympiads and other competitive exams.
