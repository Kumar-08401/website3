Who are We ?

Established in 1979 by Dr Ponguru Narayana, Narayana Educational Institutions is one of Asia's Largest Educational Institutions, consisting of 950+ schools, junior colleges, coaching centers and professional colleges across 250+ cities in 23 Indian states. With a dedicated team of 50K+ experienced teachers and staff, we enable the dreams of 600K+ students every year.

We believe that every individual has an untapped potential and all one needs is a catalyst to hasten the journey towards excellence. Our experience over the last 4+ decades combined with empathy acts as the catalyst which ensures students reach their full potential & fulfil dreams. At Narayana, education extends beyond classrooms - our integrated curriculum, advanced teaching methodologies like microschedule & personalised error analysis and specialised programmes like Soft Skills, nSports, Yoga Veekshan and Disha - Mental Wellness Programme ensure the holistic development of our students. A strong tech enabled framework consisting of apps like nLearn & nConnect ensures students & parents have 24/7 support. Trust Narayana to fulfil your dreams because at Narayana, Your Dreams Are Our Dreams.

Our Founder

Dr. Ponguru Narayana

Dr. Ponguru Narayana is the Founder of Narayana Educational Institutions, one of Asia’s largest educational institutions. Dr. Narayana is a passionate educator who has dedicated his life to the vision of affordable, high-quality education for all. He established the first Narayana coaching center in 1979 at Nellore, Andhra Pradesh. For more than four decades, he has worked tirelessly to expand his vision to 23 states, establishing over 950+ campuses. Today, Narayana Educational Institutions encompasses Schools, Junior Colleges, Coaching centers, Medical and engineering colleges, collectively educating over 600K+ students every day.

Dr. Narayana is currently serving as the Minister for Municipal Administration and Urban Development in the Andhra Pradesh government. In his capacity as a public servant, he brought in many reforms in urban development and public education that benefited millions in Andhra Pradesh.

Dr. Narayana is a postgraduate gold medalist in Mathematics and holds a PhD in Statistics from S.V. University, Tirupati.
