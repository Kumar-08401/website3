Benefits of joining The Narayana Group

At Narayana, we are committed to supporting the growth and well-being of our employees. With a strong foundation in education and healthcare, we offer a range of benefits designed to foster professional development, personal growth and long-term career satisfaction. Here’s what you can expect as part of our team:

Healthcare Coverage

Group medical insurance plans for employees and their dependents, ensuring peace of mind when it comes to healthcare needs under Group Medi Claim up to 3 lakhs under family floater.

Life & Accident Insurance

Protection plans to safeguard your loved ones in case of unforeseen circumstances under our Group Personal Accident up to 10 lakhs (GPA)

Retirement Plans

Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESIC) and gratuity benefits, securing your financial future.

Leave Policies

Generous leave structures, including causal leave, earned causal leave, maternity/paternity leave, bereavement leave and compassionate leave, to support your personal needs.
